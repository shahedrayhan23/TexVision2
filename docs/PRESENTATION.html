Key Features

Built for the Factory Floor

⚡

Real-Time Detection

AI analyzes fabric images in 1–3 seconds, providing instant defect identification with bounding boxes.

📱

Mobile App

Cross-platform React Native app works on Android & iOS. Capture, analyze, and report from anywhere.

🎯

Severity Classification

Automatically grades defects as Critical, High, Medium, or Low with confidence scores.

📊

Quality Grading

Assigns A–D quality grades based on defect analysis, helping managers make quick decisions.

📜

Inspection History

Complete audit trail of all inspections, decisions, and rework — searchable and exportable.

Works offline with local JSON DB. Seamless upgrade to Firebase for production deployment.
